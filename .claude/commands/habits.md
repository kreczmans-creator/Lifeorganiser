---
description: Show habit streaks and completion rates from your journal
---

Read the habit checkboxes from the daily notes of the last 30 days in
`07-Journal/` (get today with `date +%F`). For each habit show:

- current streak (consecutive days checked, counting back from the most
  recent daily note)
- 7-day and 30-day completion rates
- a simple sparkline like `✅✅❌✅✅✅❌` for the last 7 days

One line of encouragement max — no lectures. If a habit has been below 30%
for two weeks, ask whether to keep it, change it, or drop it from the
template.
