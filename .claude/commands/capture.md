---
description: Capture a thought/task/idea to the Inbox in seconds
argument-hint: <anything on your mind>
---

Append to the **Captures** section of `00-Inbox/Inbox.md`:

```
- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — $ARGUMENTS
```

(using the real current date/time from `date '+%F %H:%M'`).

Do NOT organise, categorise, or discuss it — capture must be instant.
Confirm in one short line, and mention `/clarify` only if the Inbox now has
more than 5 items. If `$ARGUMENTS` is empty, ask what to capture.
